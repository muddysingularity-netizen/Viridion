Education

Why Waste Matters

Understanding decomposition times and the harm everyday waste causes to our planet.

⏱️ How Long It Takes to Decompose
🧴
Plastic Bottle
450 yrs
Outlives 6 human generations. Breaks into microplastics contaminating food & water.
🫙
Glass Bottle
1,000+ yrs
Never truly decomposes — only breaks into smaller pieces. 100% recyclable yet often landfilled.
🥤
Aluminum Can
80 yrs
Takes 80 years to decompose but can be recycled indefinitely, saving 95% of energy.
📄
Paper
2–6 wks
Decomposes quickly but production deforests millions of acres. Recycle to save trees!
🍌
Food Waste
1–6 mos
Composts fast but in landfills produces methane — a greenhouse gas 25x more potent than CO₂.
💻
Electronics
500+ yrs
Contains toxic lead, mercury & cadmium that leach into soil and groundwater for centuries.
⚠️ Why It's Harmful
🌊
Water Contamination
School plastic waste enters drains and pollutes waterways. Microplastics found in 94% of tap water near urban schools affect human health and aquatic life.
🌱
Soil Acidification
Improper food waste disposal acidifies soil, killing beneficial bacteria. Schools generate ~67kg of food waste per student per year — most avoidable with composting.
🧬
Microplastic Crisis
Plastic breaks into particles under 5mm that enter the food chain. Humans now consume roughly one credit card's worth of microplastics per week.
💨
Climate Impact
Landfill decomposition and waste incineration generate significant CO₂ and methane. Every kg diverted saves 2.1kg CO₂ equivalent — small actions, massive impact.
